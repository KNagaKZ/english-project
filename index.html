Your classroom adventure

  1. Enter the names of four participants. Each participant rides one Tulpar.
  2. Click “Roll & ride”. Your horse automatically moves by the die result.
  3. A challenge opens for the space you reach. Read the short cultural text and choose the English answer.
  4. A correct answer earns 10 points and a stamp in that category. Challenge spaces give 20 points; bonus spaces give 15 and an extra turn. A wrong answer shows an explanation, without a stamp.
  5. Special spaces reveal a built-in action card. Apply it, choosing another participant if needed. Card movements do not open another question.
  6. Click “Continue”, then “End turn” to pass the dice. A bonus turn keeps the same participant.
  7. Win by completing the configured laps and collecting the required unique category stamps. Points show learning progress; they do not replace the victory goals.

Questions do not repeat in a category until its deck is exhausted. Each answer gets one attempt. Return to START and swaps preserve laps. Undo restores questions, deck order, scores, stamps and movements. You may use manual movement or teacher corrections when needed.
